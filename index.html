11 — Action Plan

Recommendations, in priority order

  1. 01

    Target the High-Risk tier first

    ~115 customers, ~$105K annual revenue in this sample, 75% average churn probability. Highest expected ROI per retention dollar.

  2. 02

    Migrate month-to-month customers to annual contracts

    Contract type is the single strongest lever the business controls directly. Even a modest switch incentive should meaningfully cut churn.

  3. 03

    Fix the support-call pipeline

    3+ support calls strongly predicts churn — this points to unresolved issues, not random attrition. Route repeat callers to a specialized retention track.

  4. 04

    Bundle tech support and security into fiber plans

    Fiber customers churn more, but those with tech support and online security churn less. Bundling offsets fiber's baseline risk.

  5. 05

    Re-score the base monthly

    Operationalize this pipeline as a monthly job so retention teams always have a fresh, ranked call list.
